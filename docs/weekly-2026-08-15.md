## Ecosystem health notes

- **Naming collisions are real**: at least two "plugin doctor" projects (zoahdev's, Xrainsmile's) and several "plugin search" projects. Verify owners before installing.
- **Quality varies wildly**: most popular projects are UI/skins/skills; verified-installable plugins with CI are rare. The catalog marks signals where audited.
- **Curation is crowded, quality-signaling is not** — this map's niche.
